## THE CATEGORY: engagement intelligence
Rezonate coined **engagement intelligence** as its category. It has near-zero search volume — so never lead with it as the *only* descriptor. Always gloss it immediately with a term people already know: "engagement intelligence platform: AI agents that…". Bridge, never orphan.

**Canonical definition — use verbatim, everywhere:**
> Engagement intelligence is AI that turns population understanding into completed action: it understands each person's context, decides the best channel, timing, and message, completes the workflow, and learns from every outcome.

**Canonical category statement — use verbatim:**
> Bots answer. CRMs record. Analytics predicts. Engagement intelligence acts, completes, and learns.

**The closed action loop** (the six beats that define the category): **Understand → Decide → Engage → Complete → Capture → Improve.** The loop is the differentiator: because the platform performs the engagement itself, it learns what actually moved someone to act — signal other systems can't generate.

**What it is NOT** (useful for comparison copy): not a chatbot (chatbots deflect, don't initiate/complete/learn), not a campaign tool (measures sends, not completions), not analytics/CDP (predicts, doesn't act), not a CRM (records the past; engagement intelligence is the system of *action* on top).

## THE ENTITY BRIDGE (highest-leverage rule)
Write **"Rezonate (formerly KeyReply)"** on every substantial page / first mention. Twelve years of KeyReply citations connect to the new brand through this exact phrasing. Founded Singapore, 2014. Domain getrezonate.com.

## VOICE PATTERNS (extends readme CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS)
- **Definitional, not promotional.** State facts plainly and early. Lead a page with a complete, self-contained answer to "what does Rezonate do for [X]" — one an LLM can quote verbatim.
- **Named entities over adjectives.** Real client names, certifications, channels, systems. Specificity beats "cutting-edge / leading" (both banned).
- **Triadic rhythm** for contrast: "Bots answer. CRMs record. Analytics predicts. Rezonate acts, completes, and learns."
- **Completion metrics, not vanity metrics.** "appointment kept", "loan repaid", "membership renewed" — never opens/clicks/deflection alone.
- **Industry H1 pattern:** "[Fan / Patient / …] engagement intelligence, [evocative tail]." or "Engagement intelligence for [the businesses trust is built on]."
