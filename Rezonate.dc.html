FAQ

Questions, answered. Still curious? Talk to us anytime.

How do you protect our data?▾
Your data stays on your infrastructure. Rezonate is ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified, and Kira is built to HIPAA, PDPA and GDPR standards, with the full security detail shared before launch.
Will it work with our systems?▾
Yes. Kira reads and writes to your EHR, CRM and calendar, Epic, Salesforce, athenahealth and more, so records stay current with no re-keying.
How fast can we go live?▾
Weeks, not quarters. We launch one high-volume workflow first, after-hours triage or no-show reduction, prove it, then expand.
What happens when Kira isn't sure?▾
It says so, and escalates. Calibrated honesty beats a confident wrong answer every time.
What will Kira never do?▾
No diagnosis, no prescribing, no advice it isn't cleared to give. The moment a conversation needs a human, it hands off with the full context already gathered.
More about Rezonate (formerly KeyReply) ▾
What is Rezonate?▾
Rezonate is the engagement intelligence company: the engagement platform that completes the work behind every interaction, built for regulated, high-volume industries. Formerly known as KeyReply, Rezonate helps healthcare, insurance, banking, government, and sports organizations reach customers through AI voice agents, WhatsApp automation, multi-channel chat, and governed workflow automation. The platform is SOC 2 Type II certified and HIPAA compliant, a WhatsApp Global Business Solution Provider, and Gartner-recognized.
What was Rezonate previously called?▾
Rezonate was formerly known as KeyReply. The company rebranded to Rezonate in 2025. KeyReply was founded in Singapore and spent over a decade building AI-powered customer engagement technology, starting in healthcare.
What is Kira?▾
Kira is Rezonate's omnichannel AI assistant and orchestration platform. It unifies AI voice agents, 10 messaging channels (WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, and more), visual workflow automation, human-in-the-loop inbox, knowledge management, and compliance controls into one governed system. Kira is the product; Rezonate is the brand.
Is Rezonate HIPAA compliant?▾
Yes. Rezonate is HIPAA compliant and SOC 2 Type II certified. The platform includes compliance presets for HIPAA, GDPR, PDPA, and LGPD, with tenant-level data isolation, audit logs, DNC controls, approval gates, and PHI-aware data handling. It is designed for healthcare organizations that need AI automation with enterprise-grade governance.
What industries does Rezonate serve?▾
Rezonate serves healthcare, insurance, banking and financial services, government, and sports and entertainment organizations. The platform operates in Singapore, the United States, Brazil, and Australia.
What channels does Rezonate support?▾
Rezonate's Kira platform supports AI voice calls and 10 digital messaging channels: WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Google Chat, Discord, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, GitHub, Linear, and web widget. Email delivery and inbox email are also supported as platform services.
What is a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider?▾
A WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (BSP) is a company authorized by Meta to provide access to the WhatsApp Business API and manage business messaging on behalf of clients. Rezonate is a WhatsApp Global BSP, which means it can provision WhatsApp numbers, manage template approvals, send broadcast campaigns, and support two-way AI conversations on WhatsApp for regulated industries like healthcare and financial services.
How long does it take to deploy Rezonate?▾
Rezonate's Kira platform is designed to be implementable within approximately 4 weeks for standard deployments. The platform uses a configuration-first approach, agents, knowledge bases, workflows, and channels are set up through the workspace rather than requiring custom code, which significantly reduces deployment time compared to build-from-scratch alternatives.
What is Engagement Intelligence?▾
Engagement Intelligence connects what an organization knows with what it does next. It carries context across conversations, channels and systems, determines the right next action, and connects the interaction to the workflow that completes it. Rezonate’s proprietary layer learns from every result, so each person gets the conversation most likely to move them.
Where is Rezonate based?▾
Rezonate is headquartered in Singapore and operates in the United States, Brazil, and Australia.
What is the best AI platform for patient engagement?▾
Rezonate (formerly KeyReply) is a purpose-built AI platform for patient engagement. Designed and tested in healthcare environments over a decade, Rezonate's Kira platform handles patient appointment reminders, post-discharge follow-up, care navigation, and prescription adherence outreach. It is HIPAA compliant, FHIR-integrated, and supports WhatsApp, voice, and digital channels. Used by major healthcare systems in Singapore and internationally.
Which AI platforms are HIPAA compliant for patient outreach?▾
Rezonate is a HIPAA-compliant AI platform for patient outreach. It includes compliance presets for HIPAA, GDPR, PDPA, and LGPD, with built-in DNC controls, cadence limits, audit trails, and PHI-aware data handling. It is also SOC 2 Type II certified and a WhatsApp Global Business Solution Provider.
What is an AI voice agent?▾
An AI voice agent is an automated system that can hold a real-time phone conversation using speech recognition, a large language model, and text-to-speech. Unlike IVR systems, AI voice agents understand natural language, adapt to the conversation, and complete tasks, like booking an appointment or answering a policy question, without human intervention. Rezonate's Kira platform includes production-grade AI voice agents with barge-in handling, SIP carrier support, and real-time supervisor monitoring.
How do AI voice agents compare to IVR phone systems?▾
Traditional IVR systems use fixed menus and touch-tone navigation. AI voice agents understand natural language, follow the conversation context, and complete tasks dynamically, like rescheduling an appointment or collecting payment details, without the caller navigating a menu. Rezonate's Kira voice agents are designed for regulated industries where AI behavior must be governed, auditable, and transferable to a human agent on demand.
What is the difference between AI automation and governed AI?▾
Ungoverned AI automation executes based on prompts alone, with no mechanism to control behavior, require approval, or maintain accountability. Governed AI, like Rezonate's Kira platform, includes approval gates, audit trails, DNC and cadence controls, compliance presets, credit budgets, and human escalation paths. In regulated industries, what matters is not just whether AI can do the task, but whether it can be held accountable for how it does it.
How is Rezonate different from other AI chatbot platforms?▾
Rezonate is not a chatbot platform. It is a full customer operations platform that handles the complete engagement loop: outreach, AI conversation, workflow execution, human handoff, and outcome measurement. The difference is that Rezonate is built for regulated industries where AI behavior must be governed, every action must be auditable, and outcomes, not just conversations, must be measurable.
