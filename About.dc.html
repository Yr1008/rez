Rezonate is an engagement intelligence company built through more than a decade of healthcare deployments. Our platform, Kira, helps organizations manage high-volume conversations, complete supported workflows and bring in staff when personal attention is needed.

Why we exist

Most systems were built to process. We were built to help people move forward.

Enterprise systems can store information, process requests and move work through a queue. They are less effective at helping a person get from a question to the right next step. Rezonate closes that gap by bringing context, conversation and supported action together across the channels people already use.

Why Rezonate, why now

Built through real healthcare operations. From answering questions to helping people complete the next step.

Before 2026
KeyReply

Rezonate began as KeyReply in Singapore and developed its platform through years of work in regulated, multilingual healthcare environments. Today, Kira supports patient-engagement workflows used across Singapore’s public hospital system.

WHAT IT TAUGHT US
Clear boundaries

High-stakes conversations require approved information sources, defined actions, escalation rules and a clear role for staff. Healthcare taught us to build those controls into the way Kira works.

2026 onward
Rezonate

In 2026, KeyReply became Rezonate. The new name reflects how the platform had evolved, from answering individual questions to helping organizations manage conversations, complete supported workflows and learn from outcomes across channels. The company remains grounded in healthcare while applying the same operating discipline to other high-volume service environments.

What we mean by Engagement Intelligence

The layer between what you know and what gets done.

Engagement Intelligence connects what an organization knows about a person with the conversation, action and outcome across channels. Kira works with existing systems to understand what someone needs, manage the next supported step and route requests to staff when judgment, verification or personal attention is required.

More than a chatbot More connected than a point solution Built to coordinate engagement across channels and systems
