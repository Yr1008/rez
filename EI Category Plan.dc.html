Owning Engagement Intelligence.

How Rezonate names, targets and defends the category. One page, five moves.

01 · Lock one definition, repeat it everywhere

The sentence: "Engagement Intelligence connects what an organization knows about a person with the conversation, action and outcome across channels."

Verbatim on the homepage, Solutions, About, Insights, llms.txt and in sales decks. LLMs and analysts quote whoever defines a term most consistently - inconsistency forfeits the category to whoever is consistent. The loop is always the same three words: Understand, Decide, Learn.

02 · Position against the categories buyers already know

The contrast: "Bots answer. CRMs record. Analytics predicts. Rezonate acts, completes, and learns."

Never sell EI in a vacuum - anchor it against chatbot, CRM and analytics budgets that already exist. The claim to defend: the layer that closes the loop inside the conversation, not beside it.

03 · Target the people who own the outcome, not the tool

EI is bought by outcome owners: COO (capacity without headcount), Head of Patient Access / fan-service / collections (completion rates), CMIO and compliance (governed automation), CIO-CISO (integration and audit). Every EI page answers all four in one screen - that is why the buyer cards exist on each industry page. Message per persona: the metric they are paid on, moved by conversations that complete.

04 · Make the site the category's reference library

Where EI now lives: Solutions hero (the emotional claim + the loop), About ("What we mean by Engagement Intelligence"), Insights ("What is Engagement Intelligence?" - the canonical explainer answer engines will cite), Newsroom (proof events), llms.txt + FAQ schema (AEO surface). Cadence: one Insights explainer per month answering a real buyer question, one Newsroom entry per cleared milestone. Never publish an uncleared number.

05 · Measure ownership, not traffic

Quarterly checks: does asking ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google "what is engagement intelligence" return Rezonate's definition; share of demo requests mentioning EI; branded search volume for "engagement intelligence + Rezonate/Kira"; analyst and press pickup of the term. When competitors adopt the phrase, that is winning - the definition they inherit is ours.
