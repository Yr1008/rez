## Who We Are

Rezonate is an AI-powered customer engagement platform built for regulated, high-volume industries. We help organizations reach people better — through smarter AI conversations across voice, chat, WhatsApp, and more — so every interaction leads to a real outcome.

We were founded as KeyReply and forged in healthcare, one of the most human and high-stakes industries in the world. That decade of experience in regulated environments shapes everything we build.

Our tagline is: **Now we're talking.**

Our core value proposition: **We Reach Humans Better.**

---

## What We Do

Rezonate delivers an AI agent platform — called Kira — that handles the full customer engagement loop: outreach, conversation, workflow execution, human handoff, and outcome measurement.

Kira is not a chatbot. It is an operating layer for regulated teams that need to:
- Reach customers at scale across voice and digital channels
- Automate follow-up, reminders, appointment scheduling, and case resolution
- Control AI behavior with governed knowledge, audit logs, and compliance presets
- Prove what happened with analytics, attribution, and outcome reporting
- Improve over time through script optimization and behavioral intelligence

### Key capabilities:
- AI voice agents with real-time STT/LLM/TTS, barge-in handling, SIP, and carrier support (Twilio, Telnyx, Vonage, WhatsApp Calling)
- 10 messaging channels: WhatsApp, SMS, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Google Chat, Discord, web widget, and email
- Visual workflow builder with campaigns, outreach sequences, DNC controls, and cadence management
- Human-in-the-loop inbox with live coaching, escalation, supervisor monitoring, and approval gates
- Knowledge management with tenant-scoped RAG retrieval and grounded AI responses
- Compliance presets for HIPAA, GDPR, PDPA, LGPD, and Brazil healthcare regulations
- Analytics and A/B testing for script optimization and ROI measurement
- Healthcare FHIR integration (Medplum, EHR connectors, Patient/Encounter/Task entities)

## Brand Voice and Positioning

**Voice:** Confident, not corporate. Clear without being clinical. Warm without trying too hard. Conversational, grounded in human truth.

**Personality:** Charismatic, engaging, informative, tirelessly helpful. "Experiencing is believing."

**What sets us apart:**
1. Forged in healthcare over a decade — not a generic AI chatbot company
2. Full operating loop — not just a messaging tool. We close the loop from outreach to outcome.
3. Governed AI — compliance-first by design, not bolted on after
4. Provider-agnostic — works with any LLM, STT, or TTS provider
5. Human-in-the-loop by default — AI handles what it should; humans stay in control where it matters

**What we are not:**
- We are not just a chatbot
- We are not a contact center platform with AI bolted on
- We are not limited to one channel or one model provider
- We are not a generic automation tool — we are built for regulated, high-stakes conversations
