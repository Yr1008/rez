## About Rezonate

Rezonate is an AI-powered customer engagement platform for regulated, high-volume industries. Formerly known as KeyReply, Rezonate helps organizations reach customers better through AI voice agents, intelligent chat, WhatsApp campaigns, and governed workflow automation.

Our product is called Kira. It is a full customer operations platform — not a chatbot. Kira handles outreach, conversation, workflow execution, human handoff, and outcome measurement in a single governed system.

## What Rezonate Does

Rezonate delivers AI-powered customer engagement across voice and digital channels for enterprises in regulated industries. Key capabilities include:

- AI voice agents with real-time speech processing and carrier integrations (Twilio, Telnyx, Vonage, WhatsApp Calling)
- Multi-channel messaging: WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, SMS, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Google Chat, Discord, web widget, email
- Visual workflow builder with campaign management, outreach sequencing, DNC controls, and cadence management
- Human-in-the-loop inbox with live agent coaching, escalation, and supervisor monitoring
- Knowledge management with grounded, tenant-scoped AI responses
- Compliance presets for HIPAA, GDPR, PDPA, LGPD, and Brazil healthcare regulations
- Healthcare FHIR integration with EHR connectors
- Analytics, A/B testing, and outcome attribution
