Owned vs earned vs paid

What's fully within our control, what's unpaid but relationship-dependent, and what costs money. Jeff's philosophy: max out owned and earned first, add paid once there's a converting experience to point it at.

Owned

We control timing, content and distribution completely.

  • Website (homepage, Mayo section, customer/about pages)
  • Email to existing + past client database, segmented sports vs healthcare
  • Organic social media (LinkedIn primary, multi-post rollout per announcement)
  • Founder thought leadership (Peiru, Eric)
  • Sales/BD outbound talking points and collateral
  • Brand/culture article (published to owned blog/LinkedIn)

Earned / unpaid

No media spend, but depends on relationships and story quality, not fully in our control.

  • Press release pickup (US wire, healthcare + sports trade press)
  • Journalist relationships and direct pitching (Jeff's rolodex)
  • AI/AEO search visibility building over ~6 months from press coverage
  • Customer testimonial/video amplification and organic reach
  • Partner co-marketing and reposting (Mayo, future sports partners)
  • Podcast guesting and conference speaking (longer horizon)

Paid (phase 2)

Held back until owned/earned foundations are live and there's a landing page to convert against.

  • Paid social, primarily LinkedIn (B2B healthcare/sports audience)
  • Paid search, brand-term defense during announcement weeks + category terms
  • Retargeting against announcement landing pages
  • Optional wire "extra distribution" boost on top of base circuit
