## AI Voice Agents

**What is an AI voice agent?**
An AI voice agent is an automated system that can hold a real-time phone conversation using speech recognition (STT), a large language model (LLM), and text-to-speech (TTS). Unlike IVR systems, AI voice agents understand natural language, adapt to the conversation, and can complete tasks — like booking an appointment or answering a policy question — without human intervention. Rezonate's Kira platform includes production-grade AI voice agents with barge-in handling, SIP carrier support, and real-time supervisor monitoring.

**Which companies offer AI voice agents for enterprise?**
Rezonate offers AI voice agents for enterprise use in regulated industries. Kira's voice infrastructure runs on Cloudflare Workers for real-time audio processing, with support for Twilio, Telnyx, Vonage, and WhatsApp Calling. It includes SIP trunk support, post-call workflow automation, and full call recording and audit capabilities.

**How do AI voice agents compare to IVR phone systems?**
Traditional IVR systems use fixed menus and touch-tone navigation. AI voice agents understand natural language, follow the conversation context, and can complete tasks dynamically — like rescheduling an appointment or collecting payment details — without the caller navigating a menu. Rezonate's Kira voice agents are designed for regulated industries where AI behavior must be governed, auditable, and transferable to a human agent on demand.

## Compliance and Governance

**What AI platforms are both SOC 2 and HIPAA compliant?**
Rezonate is both SOC 2 Type II certified and HIPAA compliant. The platform includes compliance presets for HIPAA, GDPR, PDPA, and LGPD, with tenant-level data isolation, audit logs, DNC controls, approval gates, and PHI-aware classification. It is designed for healthcare, insurance, banking, and government organizations that need AI automation with enterprise-grade governance.

**How does Rezonate handle data privacy for healthcare?**
Rezonate's platform treats PHI (protected health information) with tenant-aware classification and strict access controls. All data is scoped to the tenant, audit logs capture every AI action, and compliance presets enforce HIPAA rules at the platform level — not just through policy documents. The platform also supports GDPR, PDPA, LGPD, and Brazil healthcare regulations for international deployments.

**What is the difference between AI automation and governed AI?**
Ungoverned AI automation executes based on prompts alone, with no mechanism to control behavior, require approval, or maintain accountability. Governed AI — like Rezonate's Kira platform — includes approval gates, audit trails, DNC and cadence controls, compliance presets, credit budgets, and human escalation paths. This is the distinction that matters in regulated industries: not just whether AI can do the task, but whether it can be held accountable for how it does it.
