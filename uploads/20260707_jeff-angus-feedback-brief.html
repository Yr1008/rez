High-level direction

  • The page needs a clearer story arc: who trusts us → what changed → the proof, in that order.
  • Hero sets the frame; logos are good as-is; stories, quotes and numbers should sit together rather than in separate silos.
  • Pick 3–4 anchor customers, each with its own card: a human quote, the headline stat, and an expandable section that opens the fuller narrative inline.
  • Named attribution (name, title, organization) wherever clearance exists; organization-level attribution as the fallback when it doesn't.
  • Reduce text density overall.
  • Below the anchor cards, the full case study library becomes a filterable grid.
  • The standalone "by the numbers" section either compresses into a single thin stat strip, or — Jeff's stated preference — disappears entirely, with the numbers folded into the cards/case studies instead. His reasoning: numbers persuade better attached to a name and a story than floating on their own.
Connects to earlier work: this is the same direction as the fix already made to the repeated "94%" stat (keep client retention rate, drop the duplicate onboarding figure) — Jeff's preference here reinforces putting that number inside a card/story rather than as a bare stat.
