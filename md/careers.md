# Careers, Rezonate

> Join Rezonate and build AI that makes people feel heard. We spent a decade in healthcare; now we build autonomous agents for the front door of care.

## Key facts
- Rezonate (formerly KeyReply, rebranded 2026) — AI customer engagement for regulated industries.
- Product: **Kira**, an AI customer operations platform: voice agents, 10 messaging channels, visual workflows, human-in-the-loop inbox, knowledge management, compliance controls.
- Compliance: HIPAA, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, PDPA, LGPD presets; audit logs; DNC controls; PHI-aware handling.
- Healthcare: FHIR-integrated; reminders, post-discharge follow-up, care navigation, adherence outreach.
- Typical deployment: ~4 weeks, configuration-first.
- HQ Singapore; operating in the US, Brazil, Australia.
