# Careers, Rezonate

Build AI that makes people feel heard. We spent a decade inside healthcare's hardest conversations; now four offices (Singapore HQ, New York, Sydney, Sao Paulo) build the agents that answer them.

## What you would work on

- Voice agents answering real patient calls inside real hospitals, day and night.
- Conversations in more than 60 languages, the way people actually speak.
- Regulated, high-stakes systems where accuracy and auditability are the job.
- Work you carry from first conversation to shipped, even as an intern.

## What is it like to work at Rezonate?

Xiong, product engineering intern in Singapore: he came from a Master's in Cyber Security at NTU, was trusted to take customer needs from early discussions through requirements, prototyping and testing, found the ambiguity the hardest part of the first weeks, and says most real decisions are trade-offs between user needs, feasibility, delivery time and priorities. His advice: take initiative, and do not hesitate to hit the apply button.

## How we hire

One application (a resume is enough), an intro chat, a technical conversation about real problems, then a decision within days.
