Sliding switch — options

Three ways the agents can slide when you move to the next/previous industry. These previews loop; in the real demo the slide fires on your tap, so it lands clean with no repeat. Tell me 4a, 4b, or 4c.

4a · Continuous glide

Agents drift by in a slow, seamless carousel. Calm and premium.

4b · Step & settle

Slides one agent at a time, eases in and rests. Deliberate, in control.

4c · Slide & fade

The next agent slips in from the side while the last fades out. Soft, elegant.
