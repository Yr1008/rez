In his own words

“At school, problems have a correct answer. Here, decisions are trade-offs.”

Xiong · Product engineering intern, Singapore

What was hardest in your first few weeks?

Getting comfortable with ambiguity. I learned to ask early and trace how things actually work.

A normal day?

No fixed day. Feedback in the morning, then prototypes, demo testing, findings.

What were you doing before you joined?

Completing my Master's in Cyber Security at NTU, with previous experience in software engineering. I wanted to work on real AI products used daily, not only learn the technology in a classroom.

Something you did not expect to be trusted with?

How much ownership I was given as an intern. I was trusted to help take customer needs from early discussions through requirements, prototyping and testing.

Something class would not teach you?

At school, problems have a correct answer. At work, most decisions are trade-offs between user needs, feasibility, delivery time and business priorities.

What would you tell a friend applying?

Take initiative, work across different areas, bring a strong mindset. And do not hesitate to hit the apply button.
